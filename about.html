About Summit Snow Sports

Summit Snow Sports was created for people who enjoy skiing, snowboarding, and spending time in the mountains. The goal of the website is to share information about snow sports and encourage people to get outside and enjoy winter.

Snow sports can be expensive when you first get started, especially when it comes to equipment and clothing. Summit Snow Sports focuses on making the sport more approachable for beginners while still being useful for experienced riders.
